El Capitan Trueno

no clash patcher for the ZX Spectrum Next

How to use it

  1. Choose the original snapshot:
    Capitan Trueno, El (1990)(Dinamic)(ES).z80 (CRC32 320337B0).
  2. Press Make NEX. Your browser saves the .nex file.
  3. Copy it to the Next's SD card and run.

Details

El Capitan Trueno (Dinamic, 1990), part 1, without colour clash. The Captain, Crispin, Goliath and every enemy are Spectrum Next hardware sprites; bushes and the rest of the foreground stay in front of them on Layer 2; the scenery that clashed in the original - barrels, the bell, hanging skeletons, the well, cypresses - was redrawn by hand. The game itself is untouched and plays exactly like the original. The colour on/off item has been removed from the title menu: it no longer makes sense here. You can choose the original Spanish texts or an English translation, and switch on immortality if you just want to look around.

Nothing is uploaded anywhere: everything happens in your browser, and it works offline.

This page stores no game resources: only the changes, which can be read only together with the original snapshot. Anyone who owns the original game can make a .z80 snapshot of it and see what the game looks like without colour clash.

The mod was made as an experiment by Leonid (Leonis Silver) and Nika Claude, an AI assistant, with the nexclash toolkit by Dean (bleughb). El Capitan Trueno © 1990 Dinamic Software.
